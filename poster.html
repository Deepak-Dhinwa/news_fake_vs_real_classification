Fake and Real News

On this corpus the classes separate by house style. A linear model is already at 99%, so the perfect BERT score is not a general detector.

44,898 articlesTF-IDFBERT

Question

  • Can the writing alone tell a Reuters wire from the fake-news half of this Kaggle set?
  • The words FAKE and REAL were masked so the label could not leak through.

Method

  • Count vectors and TF-IDF with linear classifiers.
  • A fine-tuned BERT on the same split.
  • LIME for the words a decision used.

Evidence

100%BERT on this split
0.999linear ROC-AUC

What the result actually says

  • The corpus is 23,481 fake articles (52.3%) and 21,417 real articles (47.7%). Most pieces are 150–1,500 words, average about 500.
  • Count vectors and TF-IDF with linear models both reach 99% accuracy, precision, recall, and F1, and ROC-AUC 0.999.
  • The words FAKE and REAL were masked so the label could not leak through the text. LIME shows which remaining words a decision used.

What it does not show

  • Real articles in this set are Reuters copy. Fake articles come from other sites. The model can win by recognizing house style.
  • BERT at 100% on this split is one point above models that were already at 99%. It is not a general misinformation detector.

Beside the other attempts

  • CountVectorizer plus logistic regression, and TF-IDF plus a linear SVM, tie at 99%.
  • Fine-tuned BERT reaches 100% and ROC-AUC 1.0 on the same split. The gap is too small to be the story.
