Fake and Real News

On this corpus the two sides already write differently. A linear model reaches 99%, so the extra point from BERT is a check, not a new detector.

Question

  • Can the writing alone separate the two halves of this Kaggle set?
  • The real half is Reuters copy. The other half comes from elsewhere.

Why this path

  • If a word count already separates the sources, a transformer will look brilliant for the wrong reason.
  • Starting simple makes the one-point gap visible.

What held

44,898articles, 52% fake, 48% real
99%accuracy and F1, both linear models
0.999linear ROC-AUC

Reading the result

  • Most pieces are 150 to 1,500 words, about 500 on average. Length is not the trick. House style is.
  • Count vectors and TF-IDF tie. BERT reaches a perfect score on this split, one point above models that were already done.
  • That is evidence the split is easy to separate, and a reason to be careful calling it a general misinformation detector.

A real output

“The central bank raised interest rates on Wednesday after officials met in the capital.” predict.py → REAL

Why BERT was still trained

  • To test whether word order would change the story. It confirmed the split, and it did not change the conclusion.
  • predict.py scores one article with the saved BERT weights.

Scope. A detector of this corpus’s two writing styles. A new site, with a new style, would need its own check.
